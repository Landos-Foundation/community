# Testnet Participants

Anyone listed here can file a test claim, attest to one, or serve as a Land Witness under the [paper testnet process](README.md).

This is separate from governance's [MEMBERS.md](../governance/MEMBERS.md) — participating here isn't a governance vote, so the bar to join is lower. Ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/DBNpwm7MjW) or reach out to Brock directly to be added.
